## Description (original)
Anyone can ask an AI about the market. Bobby is built for the harder part: analysis you can check.

PICK YOUR COMPANION
Choose one of ten 3D companions — each with its own personality and voice. Your companion lives on your desk, talks you through every read, and evolves with you. Not with how much you trade. With how disciplined you are.

THREE AGENTS DEBATE. YOU DECIDE.
Every question runs through Bobby's desk:
• Alpha Hunter builds the opportunity case
• Red Team attacks it and hunts the risk
• The CIO weighs both and gives a verdict

You see the debate, not just the conclusion — for crypto, major stocks, and gold.

"NO TRADE" IS AN ANSWER
Most days, the best position is none. When the setup isn't there, Bobby tells you exactly that — and your companion earns Discipline XP for the patience. No signals spam. No fake urgency.

GROWS WITH YOUR DISCIPLINE
Your companion levels up — SPAWNED, LOCKED IN, MARKET READER, RISK GUARDIAN, ON-CHAIN LEGEND — through daily discipline streaks, capped XP, and honest reviews. Never through volume, frequency, or P&L. Its name, voice and tone evolve at every level.

A VOICE THAT DOESN'T SOUND LIKE A ROBOT
Warm, natural, young voices in English and Spanish, matched to your device language. Your companion moves while it speaks.

SEARCH 600+ ASSETS
Say it or type it — Bitcoin, NVIDIA, Solana, gold and hundreds more. Bobby understands names, tickers, even typos. And when a match is ambiguous, it asks before analyzing. Never guesses with your attention.

HONEST BY DESIGN
• Analysis, not financial advice — no personalized recommendations
• Read-only: Bobby never touches your funds, keys or exchange accounts
• No account required; your progress is saved on your device
• Market data from public sources

Bobby is an educational market-analysis companion. It does not execute trades, hold assets, or provide personalized investment advice. Markets involve risk.
