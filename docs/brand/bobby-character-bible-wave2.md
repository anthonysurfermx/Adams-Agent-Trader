## Why a second wave

The first ten companions are all masculine or neutral, almost entirely black
and green, and three of the six palettes in `src/lib/mascot.ts` are unused or
barely used (lava, ice, gold). Wave 2 is feminine-forward, humanoid, and each
character owns a product moment that has no face yet. The Fortnite lesson we
keep: a silhouette readable at 64 px (braid, ponytail, bob, beads, hair puff),
an expressive face, streetwear, and one cosmetic hook per character for the
CDMX / NYC / Seoul packs. Never sexualized: this ships to the App Store.

Slots 1, 3 and 5 are locked (Iris, Mira, Vega). Slot 2 is Zuri or Ola. Slot 4
is Sol or Noor.

- Proportions drifted across the batch: Iris, Mira, Vega and Zuri came out at
  realistic 6–7 heads; Ola and Noor at ~5; Sol at ~3.5 (the closest to Byte and
  Zip). Before Meshy, regenerate the five finalists with an explicit
  proportion anchor ("same head-to-body ratio as @Image 1") so the squad reads
  as one family in the wizard gallery.
- Mira's wireframe half worked as a printed texture. Keep it.
- Noor's rings and Sol's blueprint are attached to the body, which is what
  image-to-3D needs. Ola's board touches the body but is large; expect Meshy to
  fuse it with the arm.
- Naming swap inherited from wave 1: the bible calls the fox "Flux" and the
  antenna pod "Kora", the shipped files are the opposite (`kora.glb` is the
  fox). Align the bible before adding wave 2.

## Production pipeline (same as wave 1)

1. Pick the five. Regenerate at consistent proportions (nano_banana_pro, 2 cr
   each).
2. Meshy v7 image-to-3D through Higgsfield `generate_3d` (~38 cr each, ~190
   total), PBR on, no rig needed for the web puppet.
3. Draco + WebP compression to `public/mascots/<id>.glb` (target ≤ 450 KB each)
   plus a 512 px `<id>.webp` thumb.
4. Register in every list that hardcodes companion ids:
   - `src/lib/mascot.ts` `MASCOT_AVATARS`
   - `api/agent-setup.ts` `VALID_MASCOT_AVATARS`
   - `src/lib/companions/data.ts` `COMPANIONS`, `TOOLS`, `TOOL_SLOTS`, `PETS`,
     `TOOL_ART_AVAILABLE` / `PET_ART_AVAILABLE`
   - `src/components/kinetic/mascot3d/MascotScene.ts` gear `profiles`
   - `src/pages/MascotShowcasePage.tsx` role labels
   - iOS `Companion.swift` / `MascotGalleryView.swift`
   - `docs/architecture/avatar-evolution.md` `companion_id` list
5. Each new companion also needs three gear pieces, a pet, tool art and a
   voice preview line (`docs/messaging/voice-preview-lines.md`).
