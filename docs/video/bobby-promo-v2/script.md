## Anthony's concept (verbatim idea)

A person walks through Mexico City, pulls out their phone and asks "¿Qué pasó con NVIDIA?". They look
around: everyone is doing exactly the same. They are one of the crowd. Then they open Bobby and ask
again; one of our characters starts following them, like their soul, their aura, their spirit, as if
something switched on. Slogan, early access.

## Read on the concept

What works and must stay:
1. **A protagonist and a turn.** The film is about a person, not a product. The turn ("something
   switches on") is the emotional payoff a 15 s ad needs.
2. **The crowd beat is the core message, acted.** Everyone asking the same thing at the same time and
   getting the same grey glow on their faces IS "ChatGPT te responde". No copy needed.
3. **The aura companion is the app's own logic** (companion, aura, Gen Z, "Farm aura") and it is the
   thing no other market app has.

What is missing and how to fix it inside the same story:
4. **Bobby "checking the market" is not shown.** In the concept, opening Bobby immediately produces the
   aura. That shows "Bobby is different", not "Bobby checks first". Fix: one 2-second beat where the
   companion appears BEFORE the answer, turns to look at the street (the market), shakes its head at
   the crowd's identical screens, and only then the phone answers. That is "comprueba antes de
   responder", acted, and it is also the beat that shows Bobby can say "not yet".
5. **Mixing a 3D mascot into a photoreal street** risks looking pasted. Make the companion a
   semi-translucent, green-emissive presence (spirit / hologram), not a solid toy. It reads as "aura",
   it hides render seams, and it matches the "alma / espíritu" idea.
6. **Never show ChatGPT's real UI or logo.** The crowd's phones show a generic grey chat glow. ChatGPT
   is named only in the closing line (nominative use), never drawn.

## Voice (Anthony, 2026-09-03: "no tiene que escribir, la voz es un componente importante de Bobby")

Bobby is voice-first, so the film has speech, unlike v1:

| Who | Shot | Line (ES) | Line (EN) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Protagonist | 4 | "Bobby, ¿qué pasó con NVIDIA?" | "Bobby, what happened with NVIDIA?" |
| Bobby (companion voice) | 5 | "Ya lo revisé. Todavía no, falta confirmación." | "I checked. Not yet, no confirmation." |

Rules: the crowd TYPES (heads down, thumbs) and she SPEAKS (head up, eyes on the street). That
contrast is the product difference made visible. Bobby's line says both halves of the message in
five words: it checked, and it is allowed to say no. No numbers, no prices, no promises.
Seedance 2.5 generates native speech and lip sync; the lines go in the prompt as quoted dialogue.
If the generated voice is off, replace it locally with the app's own TTS voice.

## What v3 takes from Codex's V3 (2026-09-03) and what it leaves

Taken:
1. **The barrier.** The first answer moves toward the user and Bobby stops it before it is accepted (shot 5). Best visual beat of V3; it is "comprueba antes de responder" as a gesture.
2. **Three possible outcomes** ENTRAR / ESPERAR / NO HAY TRADE as chips on the card, ESPERAR lit (shot 5). Says honesty without explaining anything.
3. **Bobby's spoken line** merged with ours: "Ya lo revisé. Todavía no, falta confirmación." (checked + allowed to say no + why).
4. **The timestamp on the verdict** as the evidence beat (shot 5, card), which V3 put at 12–15 s.
5. **Message hierarchy** as a checklist for the cut (below).
6. **Name ChatGPT in the hook, never in the close.** Already how v2 was built.

Left out, on purpose:
- The agent arena with Alpha Hunter / Red Team / CIO named: mechanism, protocol vocabulary, and the v1 mistake ("habla de features").
- No human protagonist, no street, no voice: colder, and it drops the app's own identity (companion, aura, voice-first).
- Both of V3's lines: neither names the market, and the core message is locked.
- Objective "que entienda" and CTA "prueba Bobby": the objective is the waitlist.

Message hierarchy (Codex's, mapped onto our shots):

| Beat | Shot |
|---|---|
| Hook: anyone can answer | 1–3 (the crowd) |
| Difference: Bobby does not accept the first answer | 5 (the barrier) |
| Proof: it checks the market | 5 (the companion looks at the street, shakes its head) |
| Benefit: a call that is harder to fool, and allowed to say no | 5 (voice line + ESPERAR) |
| Evidence: written down before the outcome | 5 (timestamp on the card) |
| CTA: join the waitlist | 6 |

## Why shot 5 carries the whole line

The line is split across the two halves of the film: shot 3 lands the first sentence on the crowd,
shot 5 lands the second on the turn. Shot 5 must show the order (companion looks first, phone answers
second); if the answer and the companion appear together, the sentence stops being true on screen.
